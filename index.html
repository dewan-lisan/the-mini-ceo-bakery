About Me

Hi, I'm Raiyan! 👋

I started baking when I was 10 — it began with a batch of chocolate chip cookies for my family, and they said it was the best they'd ever had. That's when I knew I wanted to share my baking with more people.

Now at 12, I run The Mini CEO Bakery from my kitchen. Every cookie, brownie, and muffin is made from scratch with real ingredients and a lot of love. I take orders, bake fresh, and even deliver to your door myself.

I believe that age is just a number when it comes to doing something you love. Thanks for supporting a young entrepreneur — it means the world to me! 💛
